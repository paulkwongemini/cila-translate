“Give Them Food”

“Who then is the faithful and prudent slave, whom the master has set over his household to give them food at the proper time? Blessed is that slave whom his master, when he comes, will find so doing.” (Matt. 25:45-46) “But we will continue steadfastly in prayer and in the ministry of the word.” (Acts 6:4) In order for us to follow the Lamb and go on to maturity to become the firstfruits, we must be faithful in service in the Lord’s commission to give God as food to the members of His household so that we may win Christ as our reward in the coming kingdom. God has a household and a household administration, an economy, to dispense Himself as food to the members of His household for His expression. God has set faithful and prudent slaves over His household as household administrators, stewards, channels of supply, to give His people food at the proper time. “Give them food” refers to ministering the word of God and Christ as the life supply to the believers in the church. Christ as the life-giving Spirit is our food, embodied and realized in the word of life. In order to enjoy the Lord as our spiritual food so that we can feed others, we must pray over and muse on His word, tasting and enjoying it through careful considering. We must devote ourselves to prayer and the ministry of the word. (Experiencing, Enjoying, and Expressing Christ (4), msg. 9, III, A-C, C.1-2)

2026 FIFA World Cup Gospel Outreach

The 2026 World Cup action kicks off in Los Angeles on Thursday, June 11 with a FIFA Fan Festival at the LA Memorial Coliseum near USC. The following day, the first of eight matches will take place at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. Additional fan zones will pop up throughout the region at locations such as the Original Farmers Market and Union Station. With local residents as well as visitors from around the world gathering to watch these events, we believe this is the sovereign arrangement of the Lord for us to go forth and preach the gospel to the nations (Matt. 28:19). Our outreach, which includes Bible distribution, will be focused mainly around fan festivals and watch parties, as these present the greatest opportunity for contacting people. The first distribution dates are June 11 and 12. There will be no scheduled times during the Semiannual Training. We hope many saints—from all halls, districts, and languages—will consider joining us in this endeavor. Young people are welcome if accompanied by a parent. Go to www.churchinlosangeles.org/gospel-opportunities to see the invitation letter and schedule of outreach opportunities. Please pray that more saints sign up, including parents with young people and those who have no prior experience in this kind of activity. May the Lord prepare the hearts of many people, especially ones who are local to the area, to hear the living word of God.

France and Italy – Shepherding Trips
